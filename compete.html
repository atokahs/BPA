Competitive events

Oklahoma BPA offers 50+ competitive events across six career clusters, from accounting to video production. Events are judged two ways: objective tests (exams, production labs) and judged events (presentations, interviews, team projects). Members pick events that match their career interests, prepare through the year, and compete at the State Leadership Conference. Top finishers advance to the National Leadership Conference.
