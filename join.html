Join BPA

Talk to your BPA advisor, or email us to get started. We’ll tell you about dues, meeting times, and your first committee.

1. Register

Tell an advisor you want to join, and they will confirm your enrollment.

2. Pay dues

Pay annual dues by the deadline to activate your membership. Ask an officer for the amount and date. We don’t collect payments on this site.

3. Join a committee

Pick one of our four committees and start contributing.

4. Compete and serve

Choose events, attend meetings, and travel to state and national conferences.
