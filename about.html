Our mission

The mission of Atoka High School BPA is to contribute to the preparation of a world-class workforce through the advancement of leadership, citizenship, academic, and technological skills. Our chapter is student-led: officers and committees run meetings, plan events, manage finances, and represent Atoka at the state and national level.

Serve

Lead community service projects that make a local impact.
